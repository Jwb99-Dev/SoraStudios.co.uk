Where 2 Eat

Skills Used:

UX / UI Design App Design Branding
The Goal
The goal of this project was to design an intuitive and user-friendly mobile experience that simplifies the process of finding and booking restaurants. Starting from scratch, the focus was on creating a seamless journey where users can quickly search by location, time and group size, and instantly discover available tables. The aim was to reduce friction, improve decision-making, and deliver a fast, reliable booking experience.
