Who we are

We started with a simple idea.

We’re a small, couple-run studio focused on creating thoughtful, easy-to-use websites.

We started Sora with a simple idea—to design digital experiences that feel calm, clear, and genuinely enjoyable to use. We believe a great website should do more than just look good—it should work for your business. That means guiding your customers, building trust, and helping turn visits into real results.

We know that building a website can feel overwhelming, so we keep things simple, collaborative, and personal. No jargon, no pressure—just honest design and a process that feels easy from start to finish.
